# Prompt Flipper privacy statement

Prompt Flipper collects no personal data.

- The application makes no network calls and sends no telemetry.
- Everything stays on your device. Cards are files in the folder you choose. Settings and saved
  sanitizer rules are in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PromptFlipper`, and the rules are protected with
  Windows data protection.
- Nothing you type, copy or store in the application is shared with the developer or any third
  party.
- The About dialog has a link to the developer's Ko-fi page. It opens only when you select it,
  in your own browser, and Ko-fi's privacy policy applies to that site.

Uninstalling the application leaves your card folder and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\PromptFlipper` in
place. Delete them to remove everything the application stored.

Questions: <https://github.com/rckeller80/prompt-flipper/issues>
